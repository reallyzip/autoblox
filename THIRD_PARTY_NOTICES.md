# Inspiration and third-party boundaries

All skill instructions and scripts in this archive were newly authored for this package. No upstream code, skill files, scripts, hooks, 3D meshes, proprietary Roblox game assets or third-party plugin packages are bundled.

The workflow audit drew inspiration from public source material; mention is attribution, not sponsorship, endorsement, compatibility guarantee or an installation:

- `gamedev-skills/awesome-gamedev-agent-skills` — https://github.com/gamedev-skills/awesome-gamedev-agent-skills — project pinned revision `19d93f97c9c1ea0f8b306e77aa81e0d3e352d3e9`, Apache-2.0. Its 19 skill topics were reviewed; no upstream files distributed.
- `DietrichGebert/ponytail` — https://github.com/DietrichGebert/ponytail — MIT. Six public skill workflows reviewed and re-expressed in original Roblox production methods. "Ponytail Ultra" is an intensity mode, not a distinct upstream skill we installed.
- `Caveman` — prior Upkeep project-local skill (documented upstream revision `2e068f735253cce28256744804785f01887d9646`). The new concise-report method is original, not a copy of its Markdown.
- `Superpowers` — its 14 available skills were read through the authorized skills tool and independently re-expressed for Roblox development. No upstream files included.
- `Impeccable` — mentioned in Upkeep project AGENTS.md; its precise source was not available for inspection. The custom visual-quality method is independently authored and is not represented as a review of that upstream skill.
