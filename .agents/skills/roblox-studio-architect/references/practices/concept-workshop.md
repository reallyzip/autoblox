# Concept Workshop — Roblox Studio Architect

Origin reviewed: Superpowers/brainstorming
Activate when: Unclear or ambitious game ideas, new creative direction or major feature choices
Outcome: Reach an approved, testable game identity before autonomous building.

## Original Roblox production method

1. Inspect any existing project and the user's specific inspirations before proposing unrelated concepts.
2. Ask focused questions in a conversation rather than a massive form; separate goals, audience, genre, replay loop, camera, platforms, budget and taste.
3. Present 2–3 coherent alternatives and evaluate scope, originality, player promise, competitive identity and Roblox policy risks.
4. Summarize final decisions, rejected ideas and unresolved choices into an owner-readable creative brief and acceptance tests.
5. Ask for approval of the complete directive once; allow safe implementation autonomy after approval within its boundaries.

## Evidence required

- Approved concept brief with loop, one polished vertical slice, constraints and no unapproved speculative mechanics.
- Clear examples of what the game is and is not.

## Guardrail

Do not start coding based on a vague mood board or pretend that auto-generated art establishes gameplay feasibility.

Other skills are optional sources of inspiration; this playbook is original project guidance, not a vendored copy.
